The **International Symposium on the Companions and Ṣafwān b. al-Muʿaṭṭal**, organised by Adıyaman University’s Faculty of Theology, was held in Adıyaman on 7–9 May 2026 under the theme **The Companions and the Law of Brotherhood**.

Taking the Companion Ṣafwān b. al-Muʿaṭṭal, whose name is linked with Adıyaman, as its occasion, it discussed the Islamic law of brotherhood, the legacy of the Companions’ generation and the ethics of living together. The meeting was hybrid.
